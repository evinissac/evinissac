01 — About

A tester who genuinely
cares about getting it right.

I'm a QA Engineer at Zennode Technologies, where I test across three different product lines — WordPress and WooCommerce plugins, healthcare web applications, and Bootstrap frontend templates.

That mix keeps me sharp. Plugin testing rewards a security mindset. Healthcare applications reward attention to data flow and access control. Template testing rewards a designer's eye. I work across the full SDLC and STLC in Agile sprints — writing test cases, executing functional and regression testing, and tracking defects through JIRA.

Before this, I spent a year at RetailCloud as a Junior Product Specialist, doing manual QA on a retail inventory management system covering UI, API, and SQL-based database validation.

I'm also actively building toward test automation — I earned my Katalon Practitioner Level Certification in 2025 and I'm currently exploring where AI tools can realistically fit into modern QA workflows.

What I bring to a team: a careful eye, clear bug reports, strong collaboration with developers, and a genuine commitment to the craft of testing.
